🌙 “Ven Para Mí” (“Come to Me”) is a Russian-Spanish Nevara Latin Pop duet about attraction at night and the moment when everything between two people becomes clear without words.

A warm guitar, a soft reggaeton-pop groove, deep bass, male and female vocals, a Spanish hook and the atmosphere of a southern night tell a story that begins with a single call: ven para mí — come to me.
