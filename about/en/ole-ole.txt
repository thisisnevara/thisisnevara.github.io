🌴 “ОЛЕ ОЛЕ” (“Olé Olé”) is a danceable Nevara EDM Latino duet about a night by the sea, neon and music that lifts you higher.

Deep bass, bright synths, Latin percussion, male and female vocals, emotional build-ups and a powerful dance drop create the atmosphere of an Ibiza-style summer party: hands in the air, a sea of lights and dancing until sunrise.
