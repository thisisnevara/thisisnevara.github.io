🌊 “ОЛА-ОЛА” (“Ola-Ola”) is a danceable Nevara Latin Pop / Reggaeton Pop duet about attraction, kisses in the wind and a night when nobody needs a clock.

Bare feet on the sand, salty spray, music until sunrise and two people who want to stay together a little longer. Spanish guitar, a bouncy rhythm, male and female vocals and the catchy “Ola-ola” hook create a mood you want to take with you: on the road, to the dance floor or just in your headphones.
