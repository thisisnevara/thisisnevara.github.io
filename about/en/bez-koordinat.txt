🌴 “БЕЗ КООРДИНАТ” (“No Coordinates”) is an energetic Nevara Latin EDM duet about freedom, the sea and the road towards the music.

An Ibiza Party Mix about speed and the moment you want to leave everything behind and set off with no plan and no route.

A powerful EDM beat, Latin percussion, a bouncy bass, bright synths, festival build-ups, male and female vocals and a catchy hook create the atmosphere of a summer beach party.
