❄️🔥 “НОЯБРЬ НА ДВОИХ” (“November for Two”) is a danceable Nevara Latin Pop / Reggaeton Pop duet about meeting among the lights of the city at night and a dance that keeps you warm even on a cold November night.

Outside, the rain turns into the first snow; inside, the music plays, and this is a dance you want to last until morning. A warm male voice, an expressive female part, a reggaeton beat and melodic rap create the mood of a night where the cold stays outside the door.
