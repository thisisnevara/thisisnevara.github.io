❄️🔥 “НОЯБРЬ НА ДВОИХ” (“November for Two”) is Nevara’s new 2026 Latin Pop release about a dance that keeps you warm even on a cold November night.

A danceable Latin Pop / Reggaeton Pop track about two people who meet among the lights of the city at night and forget about everything else. Outside, the rain turns into the first snow; inside, the music plays, and this is a dance you want to last until morning.

A warm male voice, an expressive female part, a reggaeton beat and melodic rap create the mood of a November night where the cold stays outside the door.
