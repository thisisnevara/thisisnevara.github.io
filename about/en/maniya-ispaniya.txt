🎧 “МАНИЯ — ИСПАНИЯ” (“Mania — Spain”) is a danceable Nevara Latin Pop / Reggaeton Pop duet about music, glances and a night when the rhythm slowly closes the distance between two people.

A Spanish atmosphere, a hot groove, male and female vocals, light Afro and Latin rhythms and the catchy chorus “Ма-ма-мания, Испания” (“Ma-ma-mania, Spain”) create a summer mood you’ll want to come back to.
