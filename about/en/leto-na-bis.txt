☀️ “ЛЕТО НА БИС” (“Summer, Encore”) is an upbeat 2026 release about the sea, sunsets and nights of dancing that nobody wants to end.

A bright Urban Latin Pop / Reggaeton Pop duet about the last warm days and two people who don’t say goodbye to August — they call summer back for an encore.

A heavy urban bass, a swinging reggaeton groove, Latin percussion, an atmospheric indie guitar and unexpected touches of Indian sitar and tabla blend into one summer sound. Male and female vocals, seaside energy and a catchy chorus create a mood you want to keep going.
