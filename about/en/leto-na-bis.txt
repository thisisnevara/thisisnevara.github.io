☀️ “ЛЕТО НА БИС” (“Summer, Encore”) is an upbeat Nevara Urban Latin Pop / Reggaeton Pop duet about the sea, sunsets and the last warm days you want to make last.

It’s a story about two people who don’t say goodbye to August — they call summer back for an encore.

A heavy urban bass, a swinging reggaeton groove, Latin percussion, an atmospheric indie guitar and unexpected touches of Indian sitar and tabla blend into one summer sound. Male and female vocals and a catchy chorus create a mood you’ll want to keep even after summer is over.
