✨ “ЭТО ГРАВИТАЦИЯ” (“It’s Gravity”) is a Nevara Latin Pop / Reggaeton Pop duet about an attraction that can’t be explained in words.

An invisible force appears between two people — no reason, no touch, just gravity. A warm Latin groove, a dembow beat, a male rap-pop vocal, a clean female pop vocal and club energy tell a story of glances and impulses stronger than distance.
