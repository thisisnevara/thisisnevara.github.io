🌍 “ДВА МИРА” (“Two Worlds”) is a Nevara Latin Pop / Reggaeton Pop duet about family, roots and the strength to chase your dream without forgetting where you come from.

A warm Latin groove, a dembow beat, a male rap-pop vocal, a clean female pop vocal and a cinematic atmosphere tell the story of people who walked their own path, kept believing in themselves and managed to join two worlds into one home.
