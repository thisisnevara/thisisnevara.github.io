😄🔥 “ЛАТИНО-ПАНИКА” (“Latino Panic”) is a tongue-in-cheek Nevara Latin Pop / Reggaeton Pop duet about flirting and a dance where things don’t quite go to plan.

A dense reggaeton beat, a warm Latin atmosphere, male and female vocals, playful dialogue and a catchy chorus tell the story of a couple who try to dance passionately but end up in a real Latino panic.

We dance a bit crooked — but beautifully! 😄🔥
