🌙 “НЕ ОТПУСКАЙ МЕНЯ” (“Don’t Let Me Go”) is a romantic 2026 Latin Pop release about closeness and the moment you want to stop time next to the one you love.

A sensual duet about attraction at night and two people guided by music, the moon and a single shared heartbeat.

A soft Latin rhythm, a warm night atmosphere, the male vocal and a tender female chorus tell a story to listen to when the rest of the world stays far away.
