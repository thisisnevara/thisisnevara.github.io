The Spanish version of “БАМ-БОЛЕО”: a melody you already know, new lyrics and all the energy you need to dance.

“BAMBOLEO” is a Latin Pop song about attraction, joy and those nights when all you want is to keep dancing. A red dress spins to the music, eyes meet and a kiss sets the night on fire.

Rhythmic guitars, vibrant brass, Latin percussion and male and female vocals bring a party full of movement to life. The catchy “Bamboleo” and “Mamma mía” hooks take on a new life in this Spanish version. Press play and dance with Nevara!
