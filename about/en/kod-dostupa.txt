🔐 “КОД ДОСТУПА” (“Access Code”) is a danceable Nevara Latin Pop / Reggaeton Pop duet about attraction and the moment when everything between two people becomes clear without words.

Deep bass, a warm reggaeton groove, male and female vocals, a club atmosphere and the catchy “Ta-ta-tai” hook tell the story of a couple with their own secret sign, their own rhythm and their own access code to each other.
