🌙 “ДО УТРА” (“Until Morning”) is a warm 2026 Latin Pop release about dancing, closeness and a night you want to stretch out until sunrise.

A Latin Pop / Dance Pop track about the moment the night belongs only to you: city lights, a soft rhythm and a feeling you don’t want to stop.

A smooth dance groove, male and female vocals, a Latin Pop vibe and the chorus “Мы до утра не скажем нет” (“We won’t say no until morning”) tell the story of two people who lose track of time and stay in the same rhythm until morning.
